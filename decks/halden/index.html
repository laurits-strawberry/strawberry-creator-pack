The problem03

The category sells a fantasy

Outdoor brands sell adventure. Our people buy insurance.

Nine in ten jackets are photographed on a summit at golden hour. Almost none are worn there. They are worn on a bike in sideways rain, at a bus stop at 07:10, on a ferry deck in February.

Our customer is not chasing a peak. She is getting through a Tuesday, dry, warm and quietly well equipped. That is the job. It deserves better gear and a more honest brand.
