Parallel®Introduction02

A note from
our editor

Two years ago our report was about noise. Everyone had more tools, more channels and more meetings than they could reasonably attend. The working day had turned into a waiting room.

This year the data says something we didn’t expect. The noise is falling. Not everywhere and not for everyone, but for a large and growing group of people the day has gone quiet again.

This report is about who that group is, what they changed and what it costs the people who haven’t changed yet. We hope it’s useful. We hope you read it somewhere quiet.

In March and April 2026 we asked 8,412 people in 41 cities how their working day has changed since 2024.

Parallel®What happens next11

Three things we expect by 2028

i.

The status meeting disappears

Updates get written and compiled automatically. The meetings that survive are for decisions, not reports.

ii.

Managers become the bottleneck

Their day is the only one that didn’t go quiet. The best companies will redesign the role around that.

iii.

Quiet becomes a hiring perk

“Four focus days a week” shows up in job ads next to salary. We already found it in 6% of postings.
