Five parts,
four hundred hours.

    The movement

    Hand-wound calibre, 23 jewels, finished with Geneva stripes and blued screws.

  3. 03
    The hands

    Faceted dauphine hands, polished on one side and brushed on the other.

  4. 04
    The crystal

    Domed sapphire with anti-reflective coating on the inside only.

    The case

    39 mm steel, mirror-polished by hand. Water resistant to 50 metres.

Through the aperture

A heartbeat you can see.

The balance wheel swings eight times a second, 691,200 times a day. We poise each one by hand, removing metal a few micrograms at a time until it runs even in every position.

The atelier

No production line. A practice.

Our workshop on Hornsgatan has four benches and one rule: the watchmaker who starts a watch finishes it. Their initials are engraved inside the caseback.
